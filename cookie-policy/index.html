Cookie Policy

Last updated: 16 June 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how amc-engineer.com uses cookies and similar technologies (such as browser localStorage), in line with the GDPR and the Spanish ePrivacy rules (Art. 22 LSSI-CE). It complements the Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. “Similar technologies” include localStorage, which stores small preferences in your browser. Both can be either strictly necessary (required for the site to work) or optional (e.g. analytics), which require your consent.

2. Our Consent Model

By default, this Website loads only strictly necessary storage. No analytics or tracking technology is activated until you give explicit consent via the cookie banner shown on your first visit. You can accept, reject, or set granular preferences, and you can change your decision at any time.

4. Managing and Withdrawing Consent

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time:

  • Use the link (also available in the footer of every page) to re-open the preferences panel.
  • Rejecting analytics disables Google Analytics and clears its cookies on your next page load.

5. Disabling Cookies in Your Browser

You can also block or delete cookies directly in your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Note: blocking strictly necessary storage may affect basic functionality such as remembering your language, theme, or cookie choice.
